Website availability sync

When configured, this computer automatically sends the room list and current occupied dates to the public website whenever it has internet — right after any booking, reservation or room change, and again every 5 minutes in the background. This lets a visitor to the website check which rooms look free for their dates. It is a convenience signal, not a live database replica: expect a few minutes of lag, and the website still can't take a real booking — a guest's stay is only confirmed once staff speak to them directly (WhatsApp/phone). See the README for full setup steps.
